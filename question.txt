AI-Powered Telecom Network Operations Assistant

Overview

Telecommunication companies regularly handle recurring situations involving network congestion, poor signal quality, high latency, packet loss, dropped calls, handover failures, service degradation, and network faults. Over time, telecom organizations accumulate thousands of network performance and operational records containing valuable information about network conditions, alarms, configuration changes, user traffic, and previous network issues.

However, manually searching through historical network records to identify similar incidents, understand their causes, and determine appropriate corrective actions can be time-consuming and inefficient.

Telecom network professionals often ask questions such as:

• A cell is experiencing high latency, packet loss, and reduced throughput. What similar network conditions have occurred before?

• Multiple cells are experiencing poor service quality and increasing alarm counts. What factors could be contributing to the problem?

• A network region has increasing fault occurrence and declining handover success. What corrective actions should be considered?

Instead of manually searching through historical network records, telecom professionals should be able to quickly retrieve similar network cases and recommended corrective actions.

Build a semantic retrieval system that allows telecom professionals to enter natural-language queries and retrieve the most relevant historical network cases along with network analysis, risk scores, and recommended corrective actions.

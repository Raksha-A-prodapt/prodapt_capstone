### What does the Fault Occurrence Prediction Model give?

1. **Predicts future fault rate** — estimates the expected `Fault Occurrence Rate (%)` from current network conditions.
2. **Early warning** — identifies networks likely to experience higher fault levels before they become severe.
3. **Supports root-cause analysis** — the predicted fault risk can be combined with retrieved historical incidents and network metrics.
4. **Improves recommendations** — the Recommendation Agent can use predicted fault risk to prioritize corrective actions.
5. **Risk prioritization** — operators can focus first on cells/routes with higher predicted fault rates.
6. **Future extension** — with real-time network data, the model can continuously predict fault risk and support proactive network maintenance.
